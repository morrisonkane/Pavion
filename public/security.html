Security & Privacy

Practical guidance for using this safely and understanding its limits.

Protect your sign-in

Use a unique password for this . Never reuse your email, banking, or other important account password.

Keep secrets private

Do not share passwords, authentication tokens, one-time codes, or private account details with anyone.

Shared devices

Sign out when finished and avoid saving credentials in browsers on shared devices.

Report suspicious activity

Use Contact Support to describe an issue without including passwords or full payment details.

Important limitation: This should not be used to store real customer financial data. Before public launch, the operator should configure HTTPS, secure production credentials, persistent database storage, backups, rate limiting, monitoring, and a security review.

Read our Privacy Policy for the intended data handling and our Terms & Conditions for usage limits.
