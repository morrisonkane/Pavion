Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to common questions about using the PavionBank .

Is PavionBank a real bank?

This website is a project, a licensed bank and real financial service.

Are deposits and transfers real?

Deposits and transfers are records that affect balances only. No money moves.

Why can’t I sign in after registering?

New applications are pending until an administrator approves them. Rejected or deactivated accounts cannot sign in.

Who can add funds?

Only an authenticated administrator can add funds to an approved account.

What if I forget my password?

Password recovery is not connected to an email service in this demo. Do not enter your real banking password here.

How do I report a problem?

Visit the Contact Support page. The sample form currently does not send messages until a live support service is configured.

Can I use a real card or bank account?

Do not submit card numbers, bank credentials, identity documents, or sensitive financial information.
