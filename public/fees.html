Fees & Demo Balance Policy

Understand the difference between simulated balances and real financial products.

No real fees or payments

This demo does not charge banking fees, execute withdrawals, process card payments, or move real money. Any displayed balance or transaction is simulated for testing.

Demo deposits

Only an administrator can add simulated funds to approved accounts. A demo deposit does not represent a payment or a claim to money.

Transfers and withdrawals

Transfers are test records and do not settle through a bank or payment network. Real withdrawals are not supported.

No cash value

Demo balances cannot be exchanged for cash, redeemed, or transferred outside the demo.

Never send money to anyone claiming they can activate, fund, or withdraw a PavionBank demo balance.
