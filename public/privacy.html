Privacy Policy

A plain-language overview of information handled by this website.

1. Scope

This policy describes the intended use of the PavionBank website. It is a project template and must be reviewed and adapted by the operator before public use.

2. Information you enter

Registration may collect a name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored as password hashes in the demo database. Demo activity may include account status, simulated balance, and transaction records.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to registration, administrator review, login, account views, and simulated transaction history.

4. No sensitive real financial information

Do not enter real card numbers, bank login credentials, government identification numbers, or information you would not want stored in a system.

5. Storage and deletion

Data may remain in the site's configured database until an administrator removes it. The operator should establish a retention and deletion process before accepting public users.

6. Contact

Contact details and a live privacy-request channel must be configured by the site operator before production use.

7. Updates

This policy may need updating when the site's features, hosting, or data practices change.
